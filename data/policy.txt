ShopEasy Returns Policy. Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery. To start a return, log in to your account and select the order. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. Refunds are credited to the original payment method only. The customer pays for return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect. Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift cards are non-refundable under all circumstances. Damaged items can be exchanged at any outlet within 15 days of delivery. Our store is open from 9am to 9pm every day. Orders above 2000 rupees get free delivery. International orders take 10 to 15 days to deliver.
